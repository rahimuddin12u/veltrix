Privacy Policy: Last Ice

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · applies to version 1.0.1 and later

Last Ice ("the game") is made and published by VELTRIX, on Google Play (Android) and itch.io (Windows, macOS, Android and a free browser demo). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

The short version

The game does not collect, transmit or share any personal information, or any other data about you or your device. VELTRIX receives no data from the game.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It does not connect to the internet. The Android version does not even have permission to.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • It asks for no device permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or storage.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (volume, screen shake, tips) and your records (for example your best season), so the game remembers them next time.
  • Technical files the game engine keeps to run smoothly, such as compiled graphics data. They contain nothing personal.

These files are kept only in the game's own storage on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Keeping and deleting data

The files stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the game on Android deletes them. On a PC, delete the game's folder and the folder %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Last Ice (Windows).

The free browser demo

The demo runs on itch.io's website. itch.io's own privacy policy covers that website; the game itself still collects nothing, and its "Get the full game" button only opens the game's itch.io page.

Security

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no data in transit or on any server to protect. The files on your device are protected by your device's own security.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

The stores

Google Play and itch.io handle downloads and any payments under their own privacy policies. VELTRIX receives no personal information from the game.

Changes

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.

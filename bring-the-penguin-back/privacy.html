Privacy Policy: Bring the Penguin Back

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · applies to version 1.0.1 and later

Bring the Penguin Back ("the game") is made and published by VELTRIX, on Google Play (Android) and itch.io (Windows and Android). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

The short version

The game does not collect, transmit or share any personal information, or any other data about you or your device. VELTRIX receives no data from the game.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It does not connect to the internet. On Android it does not even have permission to.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • It asks for no device permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or storage.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress (the chapter you reached and the choices made in it) and your settings, so the game can continue where you left off.
  • Technical files the game engine keeps to run smoothly: compiled graphics data and, on PCs, a log of the engine's own messages (for example, which graphics card it is using). They contain nothing personal.

These files are kept only in the game's own storage on your device and are never sent anywhere. On Android they are excluded from Android's cloud backup.

Keeping and deleting data

The files stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the game on Android deletes them. On Windows, delete the game's folder and the folder %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Bring the Penguin Back.

Security

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no data in transit or on any server to protect. The files on your device are protected by your device's own security.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

The stores

Google Play and itch.io handle downloads, and any payments made on itch.io, under their own privacy policies. VELTRIX receives no personal information from the game.

Changes

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.

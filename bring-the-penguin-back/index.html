About the game

Bring the Penguin Back is a short, wordless adventure across Antarctica, about following someone you love into the unknown. Waddle, hop and belly-slide over snowfields, glacier ice and frozen lakes. Follow the prints he leaves before the wind fills them in. Call out across the ice and listen: when he can hear you, he answers, and you know which way to go.

On the way you will find the old trails, the cairns other walkers left long ago, and a stranger who needs you not to leave it behind. What you choose to do matters, and the journey can end in three different ways.

Features

  • Three chapters, about 25 minutes
  • Three endings, chosen by what you do, not by menus
  • No words, no timers, no survival meters: just the walk
  • Original music, sound, art and animation, made for this game
  • Keyboard and mouse, gamepad, or touch
  • Collects no data: no accounts, no ads, no tracking
